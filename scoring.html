Scoring sheet

How the radar scores each site, read straight from its settings. What this is explains the ideas; this is the detail.

Every site

  • Start: the height of usable lift (the part of the thermal where a glider climbs) on the site's scale, times how good the conditions are (launch wind, climb). Ridge sites start from the ridge wind.
  • Steps off (when they hold through most of the window): rain (showers −1 at most, real rain −2) · high cloud by the sun lost (−0.3 to −1) · gusts on launch over 10/12/15 mph −0.5/−1/−1.5 · crosswind −1, edge of the launch window −0.3, from behind −2 · wind at top of lift over 15/20 mph −0.5/−1 · over the back aloft −1.5 · offshore 3+ mph or LAX–DAG −3 mb −1.
  • Hard limits (a day really is out): offshore 6+ mph no-go · offshore 4+ mph or −4 mb 2 at most · gusts over 18 mph or top of lift over 25 mph too windy · strong wind (10+, gusts 15+) 2.5 at most · rain the whole window no-go · a 5 needs a breeze into launch (else 4) · Torrey under 10 mph most of the day 3 at most.
  • Models: HRRR and RRFS for the next 2 days, then ECMWF, GFS and ICON; the score is their weighted blend.
  • Day colour: the average of the two best San Diego mountain sites and Elsinore.
